## What are they?

According to [Samantha Warren](https://twitter.com/samanthatoy):

> Style Tiles are a design deliverable consisting of fonts, colors and interface elements that communicate the essence of a visual brand for the web.

You might think, "But isn't that the same thing as a page design?" Not exactly.

Style tiles are less specific. They don't outline actual page content. They focus on the common elements of a page like headings, paragraphs, links, buttons, and more. It's almost like a "theme" for your website.
