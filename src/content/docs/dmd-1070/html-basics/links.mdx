## Absolute vs. Relative Links

An **absolute link** includes the full URL (`https://example.com/page.html`). Use it when linking to pages on a different website.

A **relative link** gives the path from the current page, like `about.html` or `images/photo.jpg`. Use it when linking between pages on your own site. Relative links are shorter and won't break if your site changes domain.
