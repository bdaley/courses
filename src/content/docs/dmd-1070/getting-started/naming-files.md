description: Guidelines for naming files and folders in web development
---

In most of your other courses, the names that you use for your files won't matter very much. However, since we will publish our files to the web, it's important that you follow these general guidelines for naming your files. Not only will your file names determine how you will embed images, videos, sound, or other assets, but proper file naming can improve the SEO of your website.

## Guidelines

It all comes down to this important rule:

:::note
**Use only lowercase, alpha-characters. Separate words with hyphens.**
:::

That's it. That's the tweet.

- Do not use spaces.
- Do not use underscores.
- Do not use special characters: (e.g. %, $, \*, !, @)
- Do not use capital letters.
- Do not omit the file extension.
- Avoid using numbers. (unless part of the file extension like .mp4 or mp3)

:::caution
**Important**: Failure to follow these guidelines may result in broken links and/or point reductions on your assignments.
:::

## Examples

:::tip[Correct]
- `professor-brian-daley.jpg`
- `contact-us.html`
- `honors-program.html`
- `panoramic-view-of-horsebarn-hill.mp4`
:::

:::danger[Incorrect]
- `MyCoolWebsite.HTML`
- `my cool website.html`
- `my_cool_website.HTML`
- `awesomePageWithNumbers1234.html`
:::
