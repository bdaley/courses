## Introduction

You'll hear me refer to design deliverables as **mockups**, **flats**, **flat designs**, or simply, **designs**. Ultimately what we're talking about is a pixel-perfect representation of how your website will look when it has been fully coded and launched.

I won't spend a lot of time on this topic. Most students within the DMD program are quite familiar with design and what makes for good design. As such, we'll focus on the aspects of design that apply specifically to web design.

Please review [Chapter 8 of Web Style Guide](https://webstyleguide.com/8-graphic-design.html).
