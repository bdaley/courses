HTML Comments are great for leaving notes for other designers or developers (or your future self). But they're also great for temporarily hiding things on the page. Perhaps things that you might want to unhide in future iterations of your site.

## Example

<InteractiveCode
  html={`<p>This text will be shown in the web browser</p>

<!-- This text will not be shown in the web browser -->`}
/>
