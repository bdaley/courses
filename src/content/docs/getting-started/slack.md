## What's Slack?

We'll use Slack for realtime communication during this course. If you're not familiar with Slack, check out this quick overview video. Otherwise, skip to the next section to get started with Slack.

[What is Slack? - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHaLOoXA0vU&feature=emb_logo)

## How to Join

To join the DMD Slack group, visit <https://uconndmd.slack.com/>

:::caution
**Make sure to use your @uconn.edu email address when you create an account!**
:::

Once you're in, make sure to join the `#1070` channel. (This applies to those of you enrolled in 5070 as well.)

## Slack Clients (Optional)

It's not required that you download any of the Slack apps. You can always use the web app by visiting <https://uconndmd.slack.com/> from your favorite web browser. But if you'd prefer to have an app for your desktop/laptop or mobile device, Slack offers them for many of the major platforms.

[Slack Downloads](https://slack.com/downloads)

## Anonymous Posts

I strongly encourage everyone to post comments and questions directly into our Slack channel, [`#1070`](https://uconndmd.slack.com/archives/CFNAP8ZL6). There's a good chance that someone else has the same question that you have. However, I understand that everyone might not be comfortable posting comments or questions in a public forum. For this reason, I've installed [AnonymityBot](https://anonymitybot.com/) in our channel.
