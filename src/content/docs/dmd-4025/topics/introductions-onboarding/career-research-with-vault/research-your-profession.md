---
title: Research your Profession
description: An in-class, group, and individual exercise using Vault to research professions.
draft: true
---

1. [**Use Vault to find a job/profession**](https://uconn.firsthand.co/careers/professions) **that you intend to pursue after graduation**. (5 min)
2. **Research your job/profession**. Be sure to review all tabs and take notes. (15 min)
   At the very least, you must consider the following:
   1. What is the median salary?
   2. What does the career ladder look like? For example, would you need to work your way up to this position?
   3. Are there any requirements or certifications necessary (or recommended) for this professions.
   4. What is the career outlook for those pursuing this profession? Is the job market saturated?
   5. Are there gaps in your knowledge or experience that would prevent you from pursuing this career today?
3. **Grab your laptop and gather at a table with other students from your concentration**. If the profession you chose is not within your DMD concentration, join up with the concentration that most closely resembles your intended career. (5 min)
4. **Compare notes and present your findings to those in your concentration**. (15-25 min)
   What did you learn? It's okay if others within your concentration chose the same profession. Each student in the group should still present.
5. **Aggregate your findings in a single document about your concentration**. (10-15 min)
   You are not limited to the following, but be sure to include:
   1. Names of professions that you researched
   2. Salary information
   3. Outlook and/or job market analysis
   4. Opportunities for DMD to improve students' employment outcomes in your concentration
6. **Present the team's findings about your concentration!** (<5 min/each concentration)
