title: Show Reel Inspirations
description: Assignment — provide links to 3-4 show reel references and describe what you took away from them.
---

Provide links to 3-4 show reel references and briefly describe what you took away from the inspirations. Ideally, the show reels you choose should fit with your own area(s) of interest and will help you consider original ways of showcasing your work, your individual involvement in projects, and/or your work process.

You might want to consider commenting on:

- the pacing of the reel,
- the mood,
- rhythmic editing,
- ways the reel showcases work process,
- or how the reel draws from different projects by the creator.
