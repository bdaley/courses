The biggest project in this class is your development of a personal website portfolio that serves as a professional-level showcase of your work. You will have the opportunity to share and present your in-progress website, and get feedback throughout the semester, before the final presentation and submission of your website.

> **Not sure where to build your site?** Start with the [Portfolio Platform Research Lab](portfolio-platform-lab) — an in-class lab where you'll compare hand-coding vs. site builders, research domain names, and choose a platform.

## A Note About Domain Registration

You can look up the contact details of a domain owner through what's called a "[WHOIS](https://lookup.icann.org/)" search. That means that your personal information will be publicly available for anyone to find. Many domain providers allow you to purchase domain privacy as an add-on (See: [Name.com](https://www.name.com/support/articles/205934677-Adding-Whois-Privacy)). This will shield your private information for WHOIS searches. As such, I highly recommend using a registrar that provides free domain privacy. If you find a domain registrar that provides free domain privacy, please let me know so I can add them to this list:

- [Squarespace Domains](https://domains.squarespace.com/) (formerly Google Domains)
- [Namecheap](https://nc.me/)

### Personal Portfolio Website Assessment Criteria:

- Your own domain name
- About page/statement
- Showcase of your work
- Showcase of your work that speaks to your professional goals
- Effective design
- Cohesive design
- Clear navigation
- Responsive design
- Text is well-written and grammatically correct
- Achievement of at least two personal goals for your website (Note: if you already have a personal website that is well established, I encourage you to achieve at least four personal portfolio website goals during this class)
- Preparation for initial review of your website and portfolio materials
- Compelling statement on website inspirations
- Final presentation of your website
- Final website incorporates feedback
- Materials submitted to table of contents and submission spreadsheet on time

### Goals

As part of your portfolio website development, you will identify at least two personal goals for your website development beyond the specific criteria I have specified in the assessment criteria above. If you already have a personal website that is well established, I encourage you to achieve at least four personal portfolio website goals during this class. Examples of goals include, but are not limited to:

- Resume page/link
- Personal logo development
- Contact info/page
- Social media links (with established content on the social media profile)
- Showcase of a specific creative process in addition to finalized work
- Search engine optimization for site
- Dynamic animated graphics or transitions for site
- Revisions to an existing project that you would like to feature on your site
- Other goal that you set yourself...
