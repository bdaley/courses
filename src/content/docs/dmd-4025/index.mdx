## Welcome to DMD 4025!

This is the **capstone companion site** for Digital Media & Design BFA students transitioning from student to professional. You'll develop the core career package — portfolio website, resume, LinkedIn, show reel, cover letters, and interview skills — and build the life skills around them: job search strategy, networking, graduate school planning, and financial literacy leading up to the BFA Degree Exhibition.

**HuskyCT remains the source of record** for due dates, submissions, and grading. This site is the supplemental studio manual: video walkthroughs, frameworks, and live resources that HuskyCT handles poorly.

## Course Journey

Work follows the arc from **self-knowledge → materials → outreach → launch**. Every topic below pairs with an assignment (marked <Badge text="Assignment" variant="caution" /> in the sidebar).
