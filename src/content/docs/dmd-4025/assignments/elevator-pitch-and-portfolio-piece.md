### Instructions

You may never encounter a situation where you need to deliver your elevator pitch inside of an actual elevator. But an elevator pitch is extremely useful in a variety of situations — including job interviews. It's always prudent to have a couple of notable strengths, skills, and achievements in the front of your mind. Throughout your career, it will be essential to exercise and utilize your ability to concisely introduce yourself in a short period of time. For this assignment you will write and deliver a 30 to 45-second elevator pitch to the class.

### Assessment Criteria

Your elevator pitch should:

- Begin with a brief introduction about who you are
- Include a statement of your strengths, knowledge, or skills with supporting evidence
- Mention something that you are intensely interested in that is professionally relevant
