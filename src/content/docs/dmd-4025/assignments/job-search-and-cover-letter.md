---

### Instructions

You will identify 2 potential internships or jobs where you could see yourself working, and write a one-page cover letter for 1 of them. The potential internships or jobs you select should be currently open and advertised, and you should meet the basic qualifications for the postings. Use the job search resources below, or your own searches. Searching for jobs can be overwhelming, and this is a good time to revisit your mission statement and three-year plan so that you don't lose track of what is important to you amidst a swirl of job descriptions and required and preferred qualifications.

### Assessment Criteria

- PDF copy of job posting are provided for 2 internships or jobs that you are qualified for. (**Important**: You will lose points if you only provide links to the job/internship postings!)
- The cover letter is well-written and formatted including proper header, salutation, and closing
- The cover letter is tailored to the job announcement
- The cover letter presents a convincing case for why you are the perfect fit for the job
