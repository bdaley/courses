---
title: Putting Yourself Out There! (Goals)
description: Achieve 2 "Putting Yourself Out There!" goals to share your work beyond the classroom.
---

Throughout this course, you are spending time assembling materials that best highlight your skills and interests. But a website, résumé, showreel, and LinkedIn page are really only useful when somebody other than you sees them. And in order to do that, you have to "Put Yourself Out There!"

For this assignment you are asked to achieve 2 "Putting Yourself Out There!" goals such as:

- Contact a potential new mentor/role model/collaborator/employer
- Apply to a posted job/internship
- Create/refine your Facebook/Instagram/Twitter presence
- Create/edit/refine your Vimeo/Behance/ProductionHub/Youtube presence
- Submit your work to a film festival/competition/exhibition
- Apply for a fellowship/grant
- Some other goal defined by you...

If you would like to receive feedback about an email you've drafted, a grant proposal you've written, an online profile you've created or some other "Putting Yourself Out There!" goal, bring your questions to one of the class work days or set up a time to meet during office hours. If you want to wait till you've made more progress with your website or you've developed your showreel before sending/submitting an email, application, or other "Putting Yourself Out There!" goal, you are welcome to submit your planned application/correspondence as evidence of achieving your "Putting Yourself Out There!" goal before actually hitting send/submit. For both of your goals, create a document where you briefly describe the goal you accomplished and provide some evidence of that goal (a screenshot, link to a profile or PDF of an application, copied and pasted text from an email, etc.). If you are refining an existing online profile or portfolio please specify what you've accomplished for this specific goal.

### Assessment Criteria:

- Evidence of Putting Yourself Out There goal #1
- Evidence of Putting Yourself Out There goal #2
- Follows the submission guidelines
