### Instructions

With this project you will prepare for an interview and practice interviewing in a mock interview. You will identify 1 potential internship or job that you are interested in (this could be the same job that you chose for your cover letter project, or a different job). The potential job or internship you select should be currently open, and you should meet the basic qualifications for the posting. You will submit a written document that highlights your interview preparation for a hypothetical interview with the job/internship you select. In this write-up, you will feature five questions that you think an interviewer for the job/internship might ask you, and an outline of your response to these questions. Your responses can be in bullet point form or in complete sentences. Your first question should be some form of the "Can you introduce yourself?" question, with an answer that is some form of your personal elevator pitch. The other four questions should be developed based on the job description. In addition, your write-up should include at least three follow-up questions that you might ask the interviewers. In addition to your write-up, you will participate in a mock interview.

### Assessment Criteria

- Link and job description is provided for 1 internship or job that you are qualified for
- A single document with the following:
  - **Pre-Interview**: Written preparation featuring five questions and outlined answers (including an introductory question and personal elevator pitch response)
  - **Pre-Interview**: Written preparation featuring at least three follow-up questions
  - **Post-Interview**:
    - What questions did the interviewer ask that you were prepared to answer?
    - What questions did the interviewer ask that you were not prepared to answer?
    - What did you learn from the interview experience?
