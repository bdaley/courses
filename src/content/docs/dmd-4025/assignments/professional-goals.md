title: Professional Goals
description: Write a statement of your professional goals and a three-year plan, and present it to the class.
---

You will develop a written statement of your professional goals and a three-year plan that will help start you on the path to achieving those goals. Your statement and career plan will help you to dream big, while at the same time guiding you in the small steps you can take towards your dreams and the career you want. Your statement and three-year plan will also help you link the rest of your work in this class to your career goals.

You will also give a 5 minute presentation introducing yourself, your professional goals, and your three-year plan to the class. I am a firm believer in the idea that putting your dreams out into the universe is a big step in making your dreams come true. No better place to start than putting your dreams out to the rest of the class!

### Written Statements

#### In writing a statement of your long-term professional goals, consider what career path you would like to enter upon graduation and why. You might also want to consider the following questions:

- What are you good at?
- What are you proudest of?
- What makes you most happy?
- What makes you unique?
- What are some needs or problems in the world that you would like to help with?
- What would you like to be remembered for?
- How would you define success in your life?
- What career path(s) would you like to enter upon graduation? Why?
- What other factors outside of your career might you want to prioritize

#### **In developing your three-year plan, consider the actionable steps you will take to achieve (some) of your professional goals. Plan on answering the following questions in your three-year plan:**

- What are your current strengths and skills that will help you in your three-year plan?
- What skills do you need to acquire?
- What do you see yourself doing in 3 years?
- What do you want to achieve by the end of this year that will help you get there?
- What do you want to achieve during this course that will help you get there?

#### Formatting Guidelines:

- Arial font, 11 point type, 1.15 line spacing, 1 inch margins
- No more than one page
- Put your name and the project title at the top of the page

#### How to submit:

1. Create a document called: "Professional Goals + Three-Year Plan_[Your First Name Your Last Name]"
2. Submit the document as a Word or PDF file in HuskyCT

#### Presentation:

You will have 5 minutes to introduce yourself to the class and share your professional goals and three-year plan.

Assessment Criteria:

- Written statement includes a description of professional goals
- Written statement outlines a three-year plan with some actionable steps you will take to achieve your goals
- Presentation provides a clear personal introduction, statement of professional goals, and introduction to a 3-year plan
- Written statement follows the formatting and submission guidelines
- Presentation follows the time limit
