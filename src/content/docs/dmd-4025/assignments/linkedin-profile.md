---
title: D. LinkedIn Profile
description: Create or update your personal LinkedIn profile.
---

You will be creating (or updating) a personal LinkedIn profile. In many ways, your LinkedIn profile is like an online résumé. So all your work polishing and updating your résumé will pay off when creating or updating your LinkedIn account. But unlike a résumé, if you have a well designed LinkedIn page, potential employers and professional contacts will be able to find you instead of you needing to find them. That means there's an even higher payoff to making your profile content keyword rich. And also unlike a résumé, you have an opportunity to link to external media. That means this is a great place to include images and show off your website and showreel.

### Assessment Criteria:

- LinkedIn profile with a minimum of headline, summary, and some experience included
- Professional photograph (if you don't have a suitable photo already, use equipment from the Imagination Station or make use of the [head shot services at the Career Center](https://career.uconn.edu/blog/2024/09/06/iris-air-photobooth-crash-lands-at-the-center/))
- Clear and well-written text
- Your skills and accomplishments come across clearly
