This project is an opportunity for you to update and refine your résumé based on the resources below and input from the Center for Career Services. This is an opportunity to make your résumé a truly clear and beautiful reflection of your skills and accomplishments.

### Assessment Criteria

- 1-page, [industry-oriented resume](https://career.uconn.edu/videos/types-of-resumes/) (non-[ATS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applicant_tracking_system) version)
- Well-formatted and easy to read
- Good spelling and grammar
- Your skills and accomplishments come across clearly
- Bullet point statements:
  - Proper tense
  - Each statement communicates what, how, and why?
