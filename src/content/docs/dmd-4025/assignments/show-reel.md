---
title: Show Reel
description: Create a 1-2 minute show reel that showcases your work.
---

Create a 1-2 minute show reel that showcases your work (or update an existing show reel). Your reel should be polished and dynamic. Your reel should clearly showcase a chosen set of skills and, where appropriate, highlight your individual involvement in the work shown.

### Show Reel Assessment Criteria:

- 1-2 minutes
- Slate with title and your name at the beginning and end (added contact and website info optional)
- Showcase of polished work
- Showcase of focused set of skills
- Dynamic shape
- Individual involvement highlighted (where appropriate)
