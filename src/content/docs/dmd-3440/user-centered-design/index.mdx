## Human-Centered Design

Human-centered design is a problem-solving approach that begins with understanding the people you're designing for. It has three repeating phases:

1. **Inspiration** — learn what people need through observation and research
2. **Ideation** — generate ideas and prototype
3. **Implementation** — bring the solution to life and iterate

The famous version of this cycle comes from IDEO: **empathize → define → ideate → prototype → test**. Notice that *build* isn't even on the list until several steps in.

## Defining a Problem Space

A **problem space** is a clear statement of the user need your app addresses — *before* you decide on a solution. A good problem statement looks like this:

> "Commuters who ride the bus want to know, in real time, whether their bus is running late, because waiting at a stop without information is stressful."

Break it into three parts:

1. **Who** — the user (a persona, not "everyone")
2. **What** — the goal or task they want to accomplish
3. **Why** — the underlying need or pain point

Notice what's missing: any mention of an app, a feature, or a technology. You define *what and why* first, and let the solution emerge from there.

## Defining the Solution

Only after you've defined the problem space do you start sketching solutions. For your final project, the sequence is:

1. **Problem space** → who, what, why
2. **Solution concept** → what the app does in one or two sentences
3. **Scope** → what it will and won't do (keep it small enough to finish)
4. **Proposal** → written up as your project proposal deliverable

## React Native Focus: Reusable UI Components

As your design takes shape, think about **reusable components**. In your app, elements that repeat — a card, a button, a list item — should each be a single component you use in many places. This keeps your code organized and your UI consistent, and it's also how you'll *build* your final project: by composing small components into screens.

See [Components & JSX](../react-native-fundamentals/components-and-jsx/) to review how to create your own components.

## Lab: Final Project Concept

Create a **persona** and a **user journey map** (see [Personas & User Journeys](personas-and-journeys/)), then write up your final project concept:

- Problem statement (who, what, why)
- Your solution concept
- Initial scope — what you'll build, and what you'll deliberately leave out

**Deliverable:** your project proposal — persona, journey map, and concept write-up.
