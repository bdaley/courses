## Welcome to DMD 3440/5440!

This course introduces the processes, technologies, and environment of mobile application development through lecture and hands-on exercises. You'll design and build your own mobile app using **React Native** and **Expo** — a platform-agnostic approach that lets one codebase run on both iOS and Android.

HuskyCT will be your primary resource for assignments and course content. This website hosts supplemental material: topic guides, formative quizzes, video walkthroughs, and links to the official documentation for deeper learning.
