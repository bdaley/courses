Good apps don't fail because the code is wrong. They fail because the **experience** is wrong — people can't figure out the app, don't trust it, or don't see why it's worth their time.

This section gives you the vocabulary to judge an experience before you build it. It adapts two foundational ideas from the Interaction Design Foundation's *Basics of UX Design* (Chapters 1 and 3) to the phone in your pocket:

- **What UX is** and what a UX designer actually does
- **The 7 factors** that make an experience feel good or bad — applied to mobile
- **UX vs. UI** — how the whole experience differs from the visual surface

Work through the pages in order:

1. [What Is UX Design?](what-is-ux-design/) — the definition, the designer's job, and why it decides success
2. [The 7 Factors That Shape Mobile UX](seven-factors-mobile-ux/) — Morville's honeycomb, translated to small screens and touch
3. Then watch the video page and continue into [User-Centered Design](../user-centered-design/) to put it into practice
