This is it — you'll present your complete mobile application. The presentation has four components, mirroring everything you've done all semester.

## Presentation Components

### 1. Design Process

Show that this app was *designed*, not just coded:

- **Problem statement** — who, what, why (your Week 3 problem space)
- **Personas** — the user you designed for
- **UX decisions** — your journey map, IA, and the key choices you made from wireframes to final screens

### 2. Technical Overview

Walk through the build:

- **React Native implementation** — your app structure and main components
- **Key features** — navigation, state, persistence, API/device integration
- **Challenges encountered** — real problems you hit and how you solved them

### 3. AI Reflection

Be honest about your AI use:

- **How AI was used** — prompting, debugging, documentation
- **Benefits discovered** — where it genuinely sped you up
- **Limitations discovered** — hallucinations, verification work, where you had to take over

This is the professional practice from [Week 6](../week-06-ai-assisted-development/): transparent, verified, and owned.

### 4. Final Demonstration

The most important part — a **live app walkthrough**:

- Run the app on a real device or emulator
- Show the core user task from start to finish
- Show off the features you're proud of

## What to Submit

Your final submission bundles everything:

- **Source code** — your GitHub repository
- **Expo project** — working, runnable
- **Design documentation** — personas, journey maps, wireframes, prototype link
- **AI development journal** — your Week 6 journal, complete through the semester
- **Final presentation** — your slides and live demo

## Presentation Tips

- **Demo first, or demo early** — it's the payoff; don't bury it
- **Tell the story** — problem → design → build → result, not a feature list
- **Show the friction** — the bugs you fixed are the most interesting part
- **Practice the demo** — rehearsed demos don't fail
- **Time it** — hit your allotted time; rehearse out loud
